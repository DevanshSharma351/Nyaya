DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION PROVISIONS

CHAPTER II
OBLIGATIONS OF DATA FIDUCIARIES

4. Grounds for Processing
A person may process the personal data of a Data Principal only in accordance
with the provisions of this Act and for a lawful purpose for which the Data
Principal has given consent, or for a legitimate use.

5. Notice
Every request for consent shall be accompanied by a notice given to the Data
Principal.
(a) the notice shall describe the personal data proposed to be processed and the
purpose of the processing;
(b) the notice shall describe the manner in which the Data Principal may withdraw
consent and exercise rights;
(c) the notice shall be presented in clear and plain language, and the Data
Principal shall be given the option to access it in any language specified in the
Eighth Schedule.

6. Consent
The consent given by the Data Principal shall be free, specific, informed,
unconditional and unambiguous, given by a clear affirmative action.
(a) consent shall signify agreement to the processing of personal data for the
specified purpose and shall be limited to such personal data as is necessary for
that purpose;
(b) the Data Principal shall have the right to withdraw consent at any time, and
the ease of withdrawing consent shall be comparable to the ease of giving it;
(c) on withdrawal of consent, the Data Fiduciary shall cease processing within a
reasonable time unless retention is required by law.

8. General Obligations of a Data Fiduciary
A Data Fiduciary shall be responsible for compliance in respect of any processing
undertaken by it or on its behalf by a Data Processor.
(a) the Data Fiduciary shall implement appropriate technical and organisational
measures to ensure effective observance of these provisions;
(b) the Data Fiduciary shall protect personal data in its possession by taking
reasonable security safeguards to prevent a personal data breach;
(c) in the event of a personal data breach, the Data Fiduciary shall give notice
to the Board and to each affected Data Principal;
(d) the Data Fiduciary shall erase personal data upon the Data Principal
withdrawing consent, or as soon as it is reasonable to assume that the specified
purpose is no longer being served, whichever is earlier, unless retention is
necessary for compliance with law.

9. Processing of Personal Data of Children
A Data Fiduciary shall obtain verifiable consent from the parent or lawful
guardian before processing the personal data of a child.
(a) the Data Fiduciary shall not undertake processing that is likely to cause any
detrimental effect on the well being of a child;
(b) the Data Fiduciary shall not undertake tracking or behavioural monitoring of
children or targeted advertising directed at children.

CHAPTER III
RIGHTS OF DATA PRINCIPALS

11. Right to Access Information
The Data Principal shall have the right to obtain from the Data Fiduciary a
summary of the personal data being processed and the processing activities
undertaken, together with the identities of other Data Fiduciaries with whom the
data has been shared.

12. Right to Correction and Erasure
The Data Principal shall have the right to correction, completion, updating and
erasure of personal data for the processing of which consent was given.
(a) on receiving a request for correction, the Data Fiduciary shall correct
inaccurate or misleading personal data;
(b) on receiving a request for erasure, the Data Fiduciary shall erase the
personal data unless retention is necessary for the specified purpose or for
compliance with law.

13. Right of Grievance Redressal
The Data Principal shall have the right to a readily available means of
registering a grievance with the Data Fiduciary, which shall respond within the
prescribed period.
